First-aid kits · How to heal

When your health is low, scan the ground for a white medical case marked with a green cross, surrounded by a glowing green light and ring. Guide your character right up to the kit (within close range) and it will be picked up automatically. A kit restores up to 35 health, up to your character's maximum, and is removed once collected. If your health is already full, it will not add health. Kits are placed at several locations around the mission area, so explore safely and keep moving when you need one.
